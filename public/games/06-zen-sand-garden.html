Zen Sand Garden
🎮 Zen Sand Garden — How to Play
🎯 Objective

Rake tranquil sand waves and arrange smooth river stones for inner peace.

🕹️ How to Play
  • Drag your finger or cursor to rake soothing concentric grooves in the sand.
  • Tap 'Place Stone' and click anywhere on the sand to place natural zen river pebbles.
  • Tap 'Smooth Sand' anytime to smooth the sand for a fresh mindful canvas.
🌿 Wellness Benefit

Inspired by traditional Japanese Karesansui rock gardens to encourage mindfulness and creative expression.
