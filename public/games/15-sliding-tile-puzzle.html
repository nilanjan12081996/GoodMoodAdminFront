🎮 Calm Tile Slider — How to Play
🎯 Objective

Slide numbers 1 to 8 in sequential order on a 3x3 wooden board.

🕹️ How to Play
  • Tap any tile next to the empty slot to slide it into the empty space.
  • Arrange tiles from 1 to 8, with the empty space at the bottom-right corner.
  • Tap 'Shuffle' anytime to generate a fresh solvable layout.
🌿 Wellness Benefit

Spatial puzzle-solving absorbs your cognitive bandwidth away from daily worries.
