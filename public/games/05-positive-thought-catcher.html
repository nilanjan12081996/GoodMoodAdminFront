🎮 Positive Thought Catcher — How to Play
🎯 Objective

Catch falling uplifting feelings into your basket while letting worries drift away.

🕹️ How to Play
  • Move your basket left and right using touch drag, mouse, or arrow keys.
  • Catch glowing stars and positive words (Joy, Hope, Peace, Calm).
  • Avoid gray worry storm clouds or let them pass harmlessly.
🌿 Wellness Benefit

Reinforces psychological cognitive reframing—actively welcoming positivity while letting negativity pass.
