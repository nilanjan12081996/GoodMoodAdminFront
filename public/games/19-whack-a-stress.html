🎮 Pop the Gloom — How to Play
🎯 Objective

Tap grumpy gloom clouds to turn them into sunny smiles.

🕹️ How to Play
  • Watch grumpy gray rain clouds pop up from their holes.
  • Quickly tap the gloom clouds to pop them away and earn calm points.
  • Leave smiling yellow suns alone to keep the meadow bright.
🌿 Wellness Benefit

Lighthearted, playful physical reflex action that releases somatic tension and brings a smile.
