🎮 4-7-8 Calm Breathing Bubble — How to Play
🎯 Objective

Follow the 4-7-8 breathing technique to regulate heart rate and relieve anxiety.

🕹️ How to Play
  • Tap 'Start Breathing' to initiate the cycle.
  • Inhale deeply through your nose for 4 seconds as the circle expands.
  • Hold your breath gently for 7 seconds as the circle turns blue.
  • Exhale slowly and fully through your mouth for 8 seconds as the circle contracts.
🌿 Wellness Benefit

Stimulates the vagus nerve and activates the parasympathetic nervous system to quickly calm panic and stress.
