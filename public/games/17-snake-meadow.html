🎮 Meadow Snake — How to Play
🎯 Objective

Guide the friendly meadow snake to eat ripe apples and grow longer.

🕹️ How to Play
  • Direct the snake using Arrow Keys, Swipe, or on-screen directional buttons.
  • Eat red apples to grow longer and gain meadow points.
  • Avoid running into meadow boundaries or the snake's own body.
🌿 Wellness Benefit

Smooth, gentle movement speed tuned for relaxing flow rather than frustrating difficulty.
