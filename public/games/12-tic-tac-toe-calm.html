🎮 Calm Tic-Tac-Toe — How to Play
🎯 Objective

Enjoy a relaxed turn-based classic match against a gentle bot.

🕹️ How to Play
  • Tap any open square on the 3x3 grid to place your green symbol.
  • The computer will respond with a friendly move.
  • Connect 3 symbols in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.
🌿 Wellness Benefit

A familiar, low-stakes puzzle game that provides light, comforting mental engagement.
