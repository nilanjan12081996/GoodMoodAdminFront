🎮 Pastel Brick Breaker — How to Play
🎯 Objective

Bounce the gentle ball with your paddle to clear soft pastel brick clouds.

🕹️ How to Play
  • Move your paddle left and right by dragging your finger or using arrow keys.
  • Keep the bouncing ball in play as it breaks colorful pastel blocks above.
  • No sudden speeds or harsh penalties—just smooth, rhythmic clearing.
🌿 Wellness Benefit

Offers rhythmic hand-eye coordination with comforting arcade nostalgia for stress release.
