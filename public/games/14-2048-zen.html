🎮 Zen 2048 — How to Play
🎯 Objective

Slide and merge matching number tiles to reach the peaceful 2048 tile.

🕹️ How to Play
  • Swipe across the board or use Arrow Keys (Up, Down, Left, Right).
  • When two tiles with the same number collide, they merge into one (2+2=4, 4+4=8).
  • Keep merging numbers to reach 2048 and beyond at your own relaxed pace.
🌿 Wellness Benefit

Engages calm analytical thinking and strategic planning within a peaceful, emerald color aesthetic.
