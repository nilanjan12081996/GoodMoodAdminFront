🎮 Stress Bubble Pop — How to Play
🎯 Objective

Release tension and nervous energy by popping soothing floating bubbles.

🕹️ How to Play
  • Gentle pastel bubbles float upward across your screen.
  • Tap or click any bubble to pop it with a soothing ripple effect.
  • Read the positive affirmation revealed with each pop (e.g. Calm, Peace, Safe, Joy).
🌿 Wellness Benefit

Provides satisfying tactile engagement that breaks stress loops and reinforces positive self-talk.
