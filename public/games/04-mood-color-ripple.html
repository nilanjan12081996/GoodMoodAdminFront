🎮 Zen Pond Ripples — How to Play
🎯 Objective

Create luminous, peaceful water ripples to soothe an overactive mind.

🕹️ How to Play
  • Touch, tap, or drag anywhere across the tranquil pond surface.
  • Watch colorful glowing fluid rings expand, interact, and gently dissolve.
  • Select different calming mood palettes from the bottom buttons.
🌿 Wellness Benefit

Visual rhythm and fluid simulations reduce sensory overload and induce meditative focus.
